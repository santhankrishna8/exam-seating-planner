1. The Problem Seatly Solves

Every exam season, colleges prepare seating charts by hand in spreadsheets, tape printed lists on notice boards, and field a crowd of students hunting for their name. It is slow, error-prone, and stressful on exam morning. Seatly replaces all of that with a simple admin dashboard, an automatic seating generator, and a public search page where students instantly find their seat.

3. Admin Features

Secure Accounts

  • Register a college with name, code, admin email and password.
  • Secure sign-in / sign-out; all admin pages are protected.

Dashboard

  • At-a-glance counters: rooms, total capacity, exams, published exams.
  • Recent exams with status (Draft / Published / Archived) and quick-action shortcuts.

Room Management

  • Add halls with Name, Floor, Rows, Benches per row, Seats per bench.
  • Live capacity preview and a visual bench-grid layout as you type.
  • Edit or delete rooms anytime; capacity is computed automatically.

Exam Creation & Editing

  • Create an exam with name, subject code, date, start and end time (validated).
  • Select the rooms to use (at least one required).
  • Live capacity check — selected seats vs. number of students, with an instant warning if seats are short.
  • Fully editable later; exams can be deleted (cleans up everything, including any published copy).

Student Upload

  • Upload from CSV or Excel (.csv, .xlsx, .xls).
  • Columns: Hall Ticket No, Name, Department (optional), Section (optional).
  • Hall ticket numbers are standardised and duplicates removed, so lookups never fail on a typo.
  • Preview of loaded students before saving.

Automatic Seating Generation

Allotment modes:

  • Sequential — keep the uploaded order.
  • Random — shuffle everyone.
  • Department spread — different departments sit next to each other so no two bench-mates share a subject (deters copying).

Options & flow:

  • Gap seat — leave alternate seats empty for extra spacing.
  • Seats fill column by column: down the left column of benches (front to back), then the next column — predictable for invigilators.

Built-in safeguards:

  • If the rooms can't hold all students, Seatly stops and says how many seats are short.
  • Double-booking protection — a room already used by another exam at an overlapping date/time can't be allotted again; Seatly names the clashing exam.

Publish & Lifecycle

  • Publish to make seating visible to students (with confirmation and a re-check for room clashes).
  • Archive to instantly hide an exam from students.
  • Clear Draft → Published → Archived status with guiding banners.

Seating Map (Admin View)

  • Visual hall layout with each student's hall ticket, name and department.
  • Front/back orientation labels; shows exam name, date and timing.
  • Print-ready — clean, ink-friendly chart, one room per page, ready for the hall door.

4. Student Features (No Login)

Instant Seat Search

  • Enter a hall ticket number and hit Find Seat; Seatly searches every published exam.
  • Not case-sensitive — “21wh1a0501” finds the same seat as “21WH1A0501”.

Seat Result Card

  • College, exam name, date and timing.
  • Room, floor, bench number and seat number.
  • A visual of the bench showing exactly which seat is theirs (“You”).

Printable Seat Slip & Full Room View

  • One-click printable seat slip to carry to the exam.
  • Complete room seating list, grouped by room and ordered by bench/seat, with highlight-on-search.

7. A Typical Workflow

  1. Admin registers the college and signs in.
  2. Adds rooms (halls) with their bench layout.
  3. Creates an exam — date, time, and rooms to use.
  4. Uploads the student list (CSV/Excel).
  5. Generates seating — picks a mode and optional gap seats; capacity and clashes are checked.
  6. Reviews and prints the seating map for the hall door.
  7. Publishes the exam.
  8. Students search their hall ticket from any phone and find — or print — their seat.
