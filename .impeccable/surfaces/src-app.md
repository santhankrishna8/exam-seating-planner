Mode: Operate (admin, portal, student lookup); the landing page is Persuade-lite inside the same world.
Audience: university professors and exam-office staff at a desk; students on phones for lookup.
Memorable moment: every page opens under a green "door sign" band, and the student's answer arrives as a sign: Hall, Bench, Seat in big numerals with a yellow marker.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A campus that finds its way for you. The UI is signage: a deep campus-signage green band names where you are, big sans numerals say where to go, one signal-yellow marker says "this one". Refuses the SaaS dashboard (grey ground, blue accent, icon-tile cards, kickers) and the earlier serif press look.

OWN-WORLD: Barlow (UI) and Barlow Semi Condensed (sign headlines, big numerals), sans only. White ground with faint green tint, white sheets, signage green (oklch ~0.38 0.085 160) for page bands, action buttons and footer, signal yellow (oklch ~0.88 0.17 95) only for the current thing and primary action on green, near-black ink. Departments keep the eight-hue hood ribbon. Roundel letters and arrows as the only ornament.

STORY: A coordinator trusts the register; a student types a ticket number and is "signed" to hall, bench and seat in one glance.

Admin overview: white rail, green sign-band page header with title and primary action, next-examination sheet, schedule, register ledger, readiness.
